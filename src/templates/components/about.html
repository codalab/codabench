About Codabench

Codabench is an open-source platform for organizing AI benchmarks and competitions. Organizers define tasks, datasets and metrics, and participants submit code or results that are evaluated automatically and ranked on live leaderboards.

What you can do

  • Accept code or result submissions, evaluated by your own scoring program
  • Run inverted benchmarks, where participants submit datasets and organizers provide the reference algorithms
  • Connect your own compute workers for unlimited computing power
  • Give participants real-time feedback with live leaderboards and detailed plots
  • Fully customize pages, phases and evaluation for any problem that produces a numeric score

From CodaLab to Codabench

CodaLab was created in 2013 as a joint venture between Microsoft and Stanford University, with the goal of making computational research more efficient, reproducible and collaborative. ChaLearn joined in 2014 to co-develop CodaLab Competitions, and since 2015 Université Paris-Saclay has led the community under the direction of Professor Isabelle Guyon.

Codabench is the next generation of CodaLab Competitions, built for flexible and reproducible benchmarking. Both platforms are administered by the LISN staff.
