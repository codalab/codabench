About Codabench

Codabench is a free, open-source platform for running AI benchmarks and competitions. Organizers set up tasks, datasets and scoring programs, and participants submit code or results. Each submission is evaluated automatically and reproducibly inside a Docker image, and results appear on live leaderboards.

Submissions run on Codabench's shared compute workers by default. Organizers who need more power, such as GPUs, can connect their own workers through a private queue and keep full control over their hardware and data.

Features

Fully customizable setup

Pages, phases, tasks and scoring for any problem.

Code and result submissions

Participants submit code or predictions, scored by your own program.

Inverted benchmarks

Participants submit datasets, and organizers provide the reference algorithms.

Live leaderboards

Real-time rankings and detailed results after every submission.

Reproducible by design

Every submission runs in a Docker image chosen by the organizer.

Free storage and compute

Free storage and shared compute workers, or connect your own workers and GPUs.

From CodaLab to Codabench

2013
CodaLab is born

Created by Microsoft and Stanford University to make computational research more efficient, reproducible and collaborative.

2014
ChaLearn joins

ChaLearn co-develops CodaLab Competitions.

2015
Université Paris-Saclay takes the lead

The community is led by Université Paris-Saclay under the direction of Professor Isabelle Guyon.

2022
Codabench paper published

The platform is presented in Patterns.

Today
Codabench

The next generation of CodaLab Competitions, built for flexible and reproducible benchmarking. Both platforms are administered by LISN.
